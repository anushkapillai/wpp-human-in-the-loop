# WPP: Where Humans Stay in the Loop

**Question:** How should WPP Production redesign how an approved global campaign becomes market and channel assets, so AI handles repeatable work and people keep the judgement calls, and how much time and cost could that release?

An independent consulting-style case study using public information only. It sorts production tasks into three lanes, compares options, designs a future process with four human checkpoints, and models the time and cost effect in Excel. The central case on illustrative inputs saves about 27% of cost per asset and 36% of elapsed time.

> **Disclaimer.** Independent portfolio project built from public information. Not affiliated with or endorsed by WPP. All hours, rates, volumes, savings and risk scores are illustrative assumptions, not WPP figures or forecasts.
